# MedLex Ethiopia
### A Multilingual Medicine Information and Pharmacy Discovery Platform

## Overview

**MedLex Ethiopia** is a medical information and pharmacy discovery platform designed to help people understand medicines prescribed to them and easily find nearby pharmacies that provide those medicines.

In many cases, patients receive prescriptions without fully understanding the purpose of the medicine, the correct dosage, possible side effects, or how it should be used. Additionally, language barriers make it difficult for many people to understand medical instructions written in English.

This platform aims to solve that problem by providing a **centralized database of medicines with clear explanations in local Ethiopian languages such as Amharic and others.**

The system also allows pharmacies to subscribe and register the medicines they have in stock, enabling users to quickly find where a specific medicine is available nearby.

---

## Problem Statement

Many patients in Ethiopia face several challenges when using prescribed medicines:

- Limited understanding of medicines prescribed by doctors
- Medical instructions written primarily in English
- Lack of accessible explanations in local languages
- Difficulty locating pharmacies that stock specific medicines
- Limited awareness of side effects, dosage instructions, and drug interactions

These issues can lead to **incorrect medicine usage, health risks, and unnecessary delays in treatment.**

---

## Project Goals

The goal of this project is to build a platform that:

- Provide a **comprehensive database of medicines**
- Explain medicines in **multiple local languages**
- Educate users about **dosage, usage, and side effects**
- Help users **locate pharmacies that have specific medicines**
- Improve **medicine awareness and safe usage**

---

## Key Features

### 1. Medicine Information Database

Users can search for medicines and access information such as:

- Generic name
- Brand names
- Medicine description
- Usage and indications
- Recommended dosage
- Possible side effects
- Safety warnings
- Drug interactions
- Translations in local languages (Amharic and others)

---

### 2. Pharmacy Discovery

Registered pharmacies can:

- Create accounts
- Register their location
- List available medicines

Users can:

- Search for a medicine
- See which pharmacies provide it
- Locate pharmacies more easily

---

### 3. Multilingual Support

The system aims to support multiple Ethiopian languages including:

- Amharic
- others..

This ensures that medicine information is **accessible to a wider population.**

---

## System Users

The platform serves several types of users:

- **Patients** who want to understand their medicines
- **Pharmacists** who want to list available medicines
- **Healthcare learners** who want basic drug references
- **General public** seeking safe medicine usage information
